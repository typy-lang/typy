# Licence

## TyPy and these documents

TyPy, its source and these documents are distributed under the licence
that the `license` field of
[Cargo.toml](https://github.com/typy-lang/typy/blob/main/Cargo.toml)
names. The licence texts are the `LICENSE` files at the root of the
[repository](https://github.com/typy-lang/typy), and every release archive
carries them at its top level.

A contribution is licensed in the same way, as
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/typy-lang/typy/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
states.

## Third-party notices

Parts of TyPy come from other projects and keep their own licences.
[THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](https://github.com/typy-lang/typy/blob/main/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md)
names the runtime files derived from CPython's source and from the Unicode
Character Database, with their licences. Every release archive carries that
file, and its `licenses/` directory holds the licences of the Rust crates,
the LLVM and the Rust standard library the archive's files contain. A
program TyPy builds contains the runtime, so these notices go with it too.
